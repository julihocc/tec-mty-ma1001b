# Capstone Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Excellent | Satisfactory | Needs Work |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision question and data relevance | 15% | Question is specific, actionable, and answerable with the chosen data. | Question is answerable but broad or weakly tied to action. | Question is vague, descriptive only, or unsupported by the data. |
| Reproducible Python workflow | 15% | Notebooks run cleanly, data steps are documented, and outputs support the report. | Workflow is mostly reproducible with minor gaps. | Workflow is hard to reproduce or not aligned with the report. |
| Statistical modeling and assumptions | 25% | Methods are appropriate, assumptions are checked, and alternatives are considered. | Methods are mostly appropriate but justification is incomplete. | Methods are incorrect, unjustified, or mechanically applied. |
| Interpretation under uncertainty | 20% | Recommendation clearly reflects uncertainty, effect size, and limitations. | Results are interpreted but uncertainty is underdeveloped. | Output is reported without defensible interpretation. |
| Technical report quality | 15% | Report is coherent, concise, evidence-backed, and professionally formatted. | Report is understandable but uneven or too code-output driven. | Report lacks structure, evidence, or clear conclusions. |
| Oral presentation quality | 10% | Presentation tells a decision-focused story and handles questions well. | Presentation communicates the main result but lacks polish. | Presentation is unclear or disconnected from the analysis. |

The capstone grade should reward a well-justified modest conclusion over an
ambitious analysis with unsupported claims.
