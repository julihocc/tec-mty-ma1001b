# Assignment Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Excellent | Satisfactory | Needs Work |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Reproducible notebook execution | 15% | Runs from top to bottom and documents data paths clearly. | Runs with minor fixes or unclear path assumptions. | Does not run reliably. |
| Statistical method and assumptions | 30% | Method matches the question and assumptions are checked or qualified. | Method is mostly appropriate but assumptions are thin. | Method is inappropriate or unexplained. |
| Python data workflow quality | 20% | Data cleaning, summaries, and visualizations are clear and purposeful. | Workflow is functional but not well organized. | Workflow is incomplete or disconnected from the question. |
| Decision-oriented interpretation | 25% | Conclusion separates evidence, uncertainty, practical meaning, and recommendation. | Conclusion reports results with limited decision reasoning. | Conclusion is missing, overstated, or purely mechanical. |
| Citation, ethics, and limitations | 10% | Dataset is cited and limitations are concrete. | Citation or limitations are present but generic. | Citation or limitations are missing. |

Comments should identify the highest-priority improvement for the next
assignment. Do not only mark technical errors; also mark weak reasoning and weak
communication.
