## Decision Question

State one decision question that can be answered with statistical evidence.

Good format:

```text
Should [stakeholder] take [action] for [population or context], based on
[outcome or metric] and [uncertainty or comparison]?
```

## Stakeholder

Who would use the answer, and what decision would they make?

Name a real or realistic stakeholder. Avoid writing "everyone" or "the
company" unless the action is specific.

## Data Description

Describe the unit of observation, key variables, sample size, time period, and
known limitations.

Include:

1. Dataset source and URL.
2. Unit of observation.
3. Outcome variable.
4. Main comparison or predictor variables.
5. Missing data or quality concerns.

## Planned Statistical Methods

List the estimation, testing, modeling, or experimental-design methods you plan
to use.

For each method, write one sentence explaining why it fits the decision
question.

## Expected Deliverables

List the notebooks, tables, figures, and report sections the team expects to
produce.

## Risks

Describe missing data, bias, sampling limitations, or other concerns that could
affect the recommendation.
